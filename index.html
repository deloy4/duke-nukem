The arrows walk, Ctrl or Z jumps and Alt or X fires. Up is the action key: it opens doors, reads notes, rides elevators and teleporters, and puts keys and cards in their slots. With the grappling hooks, jump into a grated ceiling to hang from it, press Up to climb on top and Down to let go. Esc opens the menu, P pauses, M turns the sound off, and + and − change the game speed.
